# Style Selection Summary

## Staff-web style mix
**Linear + Airtable + IBM + Sentry**

Why:
- Linear gives precise, minimal structure for shell, navigation, hierarchy, and interaction discipline.
- Airtable gives excellent patterns for data-heavy grids, filters, inline actions, and structured records.
- IBM gives enterprise-grade form rhythm, accessibility, and information architecture.
- Sentry gives strong patterns for monitoring-like dashboards, metrics panels, logs, and dense operational surfaces.

Best fit for:
- dashboard
- order management
- table management
- kitchen / KDS surfaces
- inventory and stock adjustment
- menu and modifier administration
- customer support tooling
- reports, logs, audit, diagnostics
- settings and access control

## Customer-web style mix
**Airbnb + Apple + Stripe + Uber**

Why:
- Airbnb gives warm, rounded, image-friendly, approachable storefront patterns.
- Apple gives premium whitespace, polished product storytelling, and confident presentation.
- Stripe gives trust, payment clarity, polished forms, and modern transaction feel.
- Uber gives directness, strong hierarchy, mobile-first speed, and frictionless action flow.

Best fit for:
- storefront landing pages
- menu browsing
- promo/combo discovery
- table reservation flows
- pickup/delivery flows
- cart and checkout
- loyalty / membership
- order tracking
- payment and receipt confirmation
